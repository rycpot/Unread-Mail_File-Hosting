Unread Mail & File Hosting

A personal Chrome extension that shows unread mail from several accounts in one tab, and uploads files to file hosts for quick share links.

Mail

  • Gmail, Outlook.com / Hotmail, iCloud, Yahoo, AOL and Proton accounts side by side, newest first.
  • Read, reply, forward, write, mark read or unread, delete, and see Recently read, Sent, Drafts and Spam.
  • Attachments (PDF, Word, Excel, images, video, audio, text) open inside the app.
  • An offline cache on your computer, so listed emails open instantly.

File hosting

  • Drop, choose or paste files to upload them to Catbox, x02 or ImgLink.
  • Each file gets a link that is copied to the clipboard; recent uploads stay listed with previews.

Privacy

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own: your mail, sign-ins and settings stay on your computer and only go to the services you connect. Read the privacy policy.
